The essentials

Live ride tracking

Speed, distance, climb and your route, drawn live on the map. Big, rounded numbers you can read at a glance from the handlebars.

Routes, planned properly

Build cycling routes with Apple Maps directions, see the elevation before you commit to it, and keep your favourite places a tap away.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no server to send anything to. Your rides live on your phone, and your own iCloud if you choose.

Free, forever

No subscription, no paywall, no unlockables. There's an optional tip jar, and that is the entire business model.

…and the rest of the pannier

  • Bluetooth sensors: heart rate, cadence, speed and power.

  • Elevation everywhere: terrain-accurate hill profiles for rides and plans.

  • History & compare: line up two rides side by side.

  • Ride cropping: trim the faffing off either end.

  • GPX in, GPX out: import from any app's share sheet; your data leaves whenever you do.

  • Gear tracking: every bike keeps its own mileage.

  • Apple Health: save rides as workouts, calories estimated.

  • Weather on record: conditions noted for every ride.

  • Rain lock: a touch-proof screen for wet weather; hold the droplet to unlock.

  • Self-dimming screen: saves battery on quiet stretches, wakes at a touch.

  • Saved places: keep the good bakery one tap away.

  • iCloud sync: optional, and only ever your iCloud.

  • Seven languages: English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish and Swedish.

Privacy & Support

Privacy

Your rides, routes, and profile are stored only on your device (and your own iCloud, if you turn it on). AuraVelo has no server of its own and never collects your data.

To make certain features work, the app sends limited data to third parties, used only to provide that feature:

  • Apple — Maps (show the map, search places, plan cycling routes), WeatherKit (conditions for a ride), Health (save the rides you choose to, as workouts), and the App Store (process an optional tip). Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Amazon Web Services — approximate location, to look up terrain elevation for hills via AWS Terrain Tiles. Governed by AWS's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — utilized for Overpass API access to assist in routing and map data.

This data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never linked to you for tracking. Deleting a ride, or the app, removes its data from your device; anything saved to Apple Health is managed in the Health app.

Support

Users can submit issue reports through the app under Settings → Report a Problem.

AuraVelo is offered free, as-is, with no dedicated support.
